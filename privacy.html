主机、标签、设置和草稿保存在设备内。选择保存的密码和私钥凭据存入本机 Keychain，不通过 App 进行云同步。普通本机文件是否进入系统备份取决于设备和系统设置。

终端输入、命令历史读取和文件传输直接连接你配置的主机及代理。预览页面由所选远端服务提供，其内容和数据处理由该服务负责。App 不把终端内容转发到开发者服务器。

照片和文件仅在你选择后读取，副本会在本机暂存，并在你发送时上传到选定主机。移除附件或删除本机草稿不会删除已经上传到远端的文件。

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Baton. Updated October 2, 2026. Contact 3013868@qq.com for privacy questions.

Hosts, tabs, settings, and drafts are stored on your device. Credentials you choose to save are kept in the local Keychain and are not cloud-synced by the app. System backup of ordinary local files depends on your device settings.

Terminal input, history retrieval, and file transfers connect to your configured hosts and proxies. Preview pages are provided by the selected remote service, which controls their content and data processing. The app does not relay terminal content through developer servers.

Dashboard metrics stay in this app's memory. Screen Sharing travels directly over your SSH route, without uploading images to developer servers. Desktop passwords use separate local Keychain entries that you can remove on the desktop sign-in page. Mosh authenticates over SSH, then sends encrypted terminal data directly to the target over UDP.

Speech uses only Apple system recognition, requires microphone and speech permissions, and may be processed online by Apple. Recording starts only on your action; the app does not save raw audio. You can edit the transcript. Send inserts it into the current terminal and presses Return. Text input remains available if permission is denied.

Photos and files are read after you select them, staged locally, and uploaded to the selected host when you send. Removing a local attachment or draft does not delete files already uploaded to a remote host.

This build has no ads, tracking SDKs, app accounts, or developer analytics service. If you install remote tools, the app may download resources from GitHub or the tool website, which receives ordinary network request information. TestFlight may separately share crashes and test feedback with Apple and the developer; see Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

You can delete hosts, attachments, and drafts in the app, revoke permissions in system settings, and disable Live Activities. Live Activities may show host and session names. Manage remote data on the relevant server. Remove passwords, private keys, and sensitive terminal content before emailing support.

When task alerts are enabled, push tokens, device and pairing identifiers, alert preferences, tool type, normalized task status and event times are sent to our notification service and delivered through Apple APNs. Events are linked to the device for alerts, synchronization and troubleshooting. Prompts, code, terminal output, full commands, working directories and error stacks are not uploaded.

Apple processes purchases; we do not receive payment card details. Task alerts are free and require no Apple Account sign-in, Pro purchase, or trial verification. New versions no longer read or upload Apple app, purchase, or App Attest proofs for task alerts. Making task alerts free does not itself erase existing verification records. Device cleanup conditions are described below; contact support for other deletion requests.

When a notification device is registered, we link a SHA-256 hash of its source IP address to the device record to limit abusive registrations. Scheduled maintenance clears that field after the device record is one hour old. The notification service also retains operational diagnostics such as fixed routes, response status, request size and error categories. Some warnings or errors at the entry proxy may record the source IP address, request URI, User-Agent and time. This request-level information may be linked to a user or device and is not treated as anonymous statistics. It is used only for service operation, security and troubleshooting, not advertising or tracking.

Task events, test notifications and verification challenges are removed by scheduled maintenance after they are 24 hours old. A device is automatically deleted only when it has not re-registered for more than seven days, its purchase and trial entitlements have expired, it has no pairings and it has no still-valid entitlement verification source. Deletion also removes the associated entitlement sources and device-to-trial links; the separate historical trial ledger has no automatic expiry cleanup. Removing a host notification connection revokes its pairing and deletes associated subscriptions and events, but does not erase all server data. Disabling system notifications does not delete server data either.

The 24-hour, one-hour and seven-day rules above do not apply to operational logs or backups. Notification application and database logs rotate by size rather than expiring after a fixed number of days. Entry-proxy logs currently have no configured automatic expiry or size-rotation limit. Backups may retain data that has been removed from the active database. Contact support about complete data deletion and retention in logs or backups; disabling permissions or deleting a pairing does not immediately erase every copy.

Live Activity tokens, host and session display names, and your focus choice are also sent when Lock Screen task status is enabled. Activity registrations are deleted after seven days without an update.

写真やファイルは選択後に読み込み、端末に一時保存し、送信時に選択したホストへアップロードします。端末の添付ファイルや下書きを削除しても、転送済みのリモートファイルは削除されません。

購入は Apple が処理し、当社はカード情報を受け取りません。タスク通知は無料で、Apple Account へのサインイン、Pro の購入、試用資格の確認は不要です。新しいバージョンはタスク通知のために Apple の App・購入・App Attest 証明を読み取り・送信しません。タスク通知の無料化だけでは既存の検証記録は消去されません。デバイスの削除条件は以下のとおりです。その他の削除依頼はサポートへお問い合わせください。

通知用デバイスの登録時には、不正な登録を制限するため、送信元 IP アドレスの SHA-256 ハッシュをデバイス記録に関連付けます。記録の作成から1時間経過後、定期メンテナンスでこの項目を消去します。通知サービスは固定ルート、応答ステータス、リクエストサイズ、エラー分類などの運用診断も保持します。入口プロキシの一部の警告・エラーログには、送信元 IP アドレス、リクエスト URI、User-Agent、時刻が記録される場合があります。これらのリクエスト単位の情報はユーザーやデバイスに関連付けられる可能性があり、匿名の統計としては扱いません。サービス運用、セキュリティ、障害調査のみに使用し、広告や追跡には使用しません。

Los hosts, pestañas, ajustes y borradores se guardan en tu dispositivo. Las credenciales que decides guardar se almacenan en el Keychain local y la app no las sincroniza en la nube. La copia de seguridad de otros archivos depende de los ajustes del dispositivo.

La entrada del terminal, el historial y las transferencias se conectan a tus hosts y proxies. El servicio remoto elegido controla las páginas de vista previa y su tratamiento de datos. La app no retransmite el contenido del terminal por servidores del desarrollador.
